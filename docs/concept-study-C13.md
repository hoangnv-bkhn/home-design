## Alignment and usability

Their clear sides, the private wet bay and the ground kitchen/upper spare and Grandpa/sister divisions now follow the same principal x references. The four x lines give nominal reference spans **3.95 / 2.60 / 4.15 m**. They are not an equal-spacing grid. The court/window openings, stair opening and terrace recess still interrupt potential framing.
