The front reads as two enclosed upper volumes around an outdoor recess, with a lighter **2.30 × 2.50 m porch-canopy candidate** below. Massing and F2 roof outline show the same notch. The canopy covers the modeled porch rectangle in plan; it does not establish weather protection in wind or a designed cantilever.

**Room cantilever considered:** the sister's front room is the logical optional 0.40–0.60 m projection for a later elevation study, because it faces D and can relate to the porch/loggia. These choices keep layering tied to useful spaces.

## What the wall alignment means structurally

The observed mismatch is real, but it is **not by itself evidence of unsafe or incorrect beam design**. A load-bearing wall and a non-load-bearing partition perform different roles. C10 uses alignment as an architectural coordination improvement; it does not assume every room wall must sit on a beam or that aligned walls establish a continuous load path.

General evidence, accessed **2026-10-03**: The Concrete Centre, [Building elements — Walls and Transfer slabs](https://www.concretecentre.com/Structural-design/Building-Elements.aspx), undated web guidance, no numbered clause/edition. It defines load-bearing walls as carrying roof/floor loads to foundations and identifies transfer slabs with changes in column layout between floors. Canada Masonry Design Centre, [Partition Wall Design Guides](https://www.canadamasonrydesigncentre.com/resources/partitions/guides/), Version 1.1 landing-page introduction (no numbered clause), explicitly addresses non-load-bearing masonry partitions and project-specific professional review. Its Canadian standards are not adopted for this house; no guide tables or calculations used. This is general background, **not a Vietnamese code, project structural design or proof that this house needs/avoids transfer beams**.

Project inference: keep the new x=3.65 line and existing x=6.25 line as discussion axes; ask the engineer to select the actual RC/masonry system, columns, spans, slab/wall loads and foundations, then review the court, stair opening and recessed balcony edges together. No member sizes, reinforcement, cantilever capacity or construction clearance has been established.
