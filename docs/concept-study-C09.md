## What the feedback revealed

The buffer had a nominal 0.70 m door, but the sofa blocked its approach. A door symbol alone did not establish access; earlier checks missed this relationship. The shower-side wall centerline was x=6.25 m while the stair/Grandpa division was x=5.95 m: a real 300 mm offset. The upper 12.80 m² landing had no furnished purpose, alongside the family's required 10.20 m² empty altar projection.

C09 repairs the access and wall coordination and gives the upper common area a defined use. This is a usability revision, **not a reduction in the 119.20 m² covered footprint**. The required altar exclusion remains a substantial area commitment.

## What wall alignment does and does not answer

The drawing confirms the offset the owner noticed. C09 removes that offset and stacks the revised bedroom divisions. This is a clearer architectural coordination proposal for structural review. It does **not** establish that C08 was unsafe or that C09 has adequate beams. No structural system, wall weights, column positions, member sizes, soil basis or load calculations have been adopted.

The grid overlay shows reference wall axes, including the remaining 0.30 m change between the rear bedroom/wet core boundary and the stair/kitchen boundary. It is deliberately **not a regularized engineered frame**. E01 must resolve spans, supports at corridor openings, the stair opening, court edges and the projecting balcony together. No column symbols or beam sizes are invented to imply completion.
