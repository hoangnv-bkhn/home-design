## Sanitary fit and circulation

The narrower compartments save **3.90 m² per floor**, or 7.80 m² across both floors, compared with the C03 wet-room rectangles (13.02 → 9.12 m² per floor). The saving supports the revised suite/landing arrangement; it is not a gross-floor-area saving.

WCs retain 0.55 × 0.70 m fixture reservations and no basins. Each shower has a 0.90 × 0.90 m tray at one end and a compact 0.45 m basin reservation farther along its depth, rather than assuming both fit side by side in 1.40 m. The 1.40 × 1.80 m shared shower is the tightest study. Door gaps are 0.75/0.80 m reservations; sliding/outward doors are candidates, with pocket length, leaves, standing clearance and fixture choices unresolved. These are **finished-clear concept dimensions**, not external wall dimensions, verified standards or a universal suitability claim. No special accessibility brief is assumed.

Grandpa's route reaches shared bathrooms through common living/access space, without entering a bedroom or parents' suite. The relocated door is a spatial privacy improvement to review; TV sound transmission and occupied clearances are not resolved. His 2.8 × 5.2 m room, and sister's 2.8 × 6.0 m room, become longer/narrower. Furniture rectangles show reservations; they do not prove convenient use. Six dining chairs are drawn; chair pull-out, garden passage and appliance use remain L05/L07.
