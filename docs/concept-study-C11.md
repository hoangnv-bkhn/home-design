# C11 — aligned rooms, open right terrace and a composed entrance

2026-10-04. Assistant design proposal, unselected; not for construction. [Viewer](../outputs/house-concepts.html) · [F1](../outputs/option-01-F1.svg) · [F2](../outputs/option-01-F2.svg) · [Open terrace exterior](../outputs/option-01-massing.svg) · [Sheltered exterior](../outputs/option-02-massing.svg) · [Sections](../outputs/option-02-section.svg).

The owner asks for better wall/grid alignment, less unnecessary enclosure, a larger right-side balcony replacing the study, a wider stair where useful, a proper entrance composition and a more considered exterior. These are directions for iteration, not acceptance of the dimensions below. The former 2.20 m stair width is no longer a fixed requirement. C10 was preserved and its 56-file archive verified before source changes.

## Wall alignment and the private dressing space

The shared WC/shower moves **300 mm toward C (left on the displayed plan)**. Its rear wall now aligns with the parents/brother wall at local y=4.25 m. The private dressing enclosure extends to the same line, removing the small offset between the suite and shared wet rooms. All four pairs retain separate WC and shower compartments; every shower retains its basin. Wet rooms stack exactly between floors.

The stair expands into the unused 300 mm strip: **2.50 × 3.70 m**, with two nominal **1.10 m flights**, a 0.30 m central gap/rail reservation and 1.10 m intermediate landing. The 21-riser/260 mm going concept is retained. The stair's x=3.65 wall now aligns with the suite, kitchen and spare bedroom; x=6.25 remains the opposite wet/stair/Grandpa-sister line. Nominal bay dimensions are not finished clear widths or a headroom check.

These are useful shared wall references, not a demand that every room fit a uniform structural grid. The court edge, altar backing and facade have legitimate separate lines. An engineer must establish supports and load paths; adding grid lines does not establish a structural system.

The full dividing wall between the two private dressing/turning zones is removed. Their IDs remain separate for continuity, but they form one space. **The short transverse privacy screen stays**: removing that as well would expose bathroom-door sightlines and discard the requested screened turn. The outer enclosure and ordinary bedroom entrance remain. The enlarged private zone is 2.50 × 2.10 m before the short screen; no extra wardrobe is forced into its circulation. Sampled bed-to-door sightlines pass, but all-position privacy, dressing and simultaneous door use remain open.

## Open terrace in place of the study

The former study and right forward landing become a **2.30 × 4.60 m / 10.58 m² shared terrace**. Its 1.70 m clear recess and 0.60 m outward projection give useful depth without a large room cantilever. The old central balcony recess closes into the common landing. A 1.20 m sliding weather-door candidate enters the terrace from shared circulation, independent of bedrooms.

The terrace ends before the main entrance's lateral band: local y=0.20–4.80 versus entry y=5.40–7.30. It is now visibly on the right rather than centered above the entrance. The **4.08 m² empty altar strip remains indoors**, behind the terrace's weather wall; neither furniture nor the terrace route crosses it. The solid ground altar backing and worship floor remain.

Open vertical metal guards replace the old floor-to-roof balcony frame. The illustrated 1.10 m guard is a placeholder, not a code finding. There is still a necessary weather wall behind the terrace and another at its shared entrance. The near-A side is more exposed to neighbors: privacy, overlooking and permission need site-specific review; an open terrace is not evidence of a right to open the boundary.

Both options use the same floor geometry and area:

| Option | Exterior treatment | Practical consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Light slatted pergola over a 2.40 m-wide portion | More open sky; partial shade only, no rain shelter promised |
| 02 | Thin roof over the full terrace | More useful shelter for sitting; additional roof, posts, drainage and wind design |

My preference for further development is **Option 02**, retaining its open sides. This is a design judgment, not owner selection or a verified shade/rain result. A 1.60 m bench faces toward the altar side with its back away; the sampled 0.80 m route passes beside the occupied bench footprint. Two small shade-post reservations fit inside the slab and outside that route. The remaining area is deliberately left flexible rather than crowded with a dining set.

## Quiet buffer and linen alcove

The ground buffer becomes a **doorless quiet display alcove**. Keep its 0.80 m side opening, 0.90 m clear width and 0.95 m approach behind the sofa. A shallow 0.25 m ledge at the dead end can hold a modest dry display. Seating, deep shelving and frequent circulation would make this narrow space less useful, so none is proposed. The solid altar backing stays uninterrupted; this does not adopt an outdoor gap or add a cultural rule.

Upstairs, remove the linen alcove door and the partition separating it from the empty altar strip. Linen remains in a **closed cabinet**, reached along the former 0.90 m aisle; the empty strip is distinguished by a floor border. This avoids two narrow enclosed compartments. The alcove remains low-use maintenance space; it is not counted as another useful room. The family must review the proposed display use and open upper arrangement under its existing interpretation.

## Entrance and exterior

The exterior now has a recessed right terrace, a pale upper volume, a charcoal surround joining the two stair windows, a generous landing window above the main entrance, and a warm timber entry. The landing window's proposed low fixed-light portion needs protective glazing/guard design. Thin window shades and the entrance canopy provide secondary depth; heavy terrace side frames are removed. Materials are concept choices, not specified products.

The entrance is shown in plan, site, massing and section:

- **2.20 × 2.20 m porch**, retaining the outward door leaves and 1.15 m front waiting strip.
- **2.45 × 2.80 m canopy**, 2.70 m proposed soffit and 0.12 m visual edge; two outer posts sit outside the door sweeps, waiting space and arrival band.
- **Three 150 mm rises** from assumed yard −0.45 m to porch ±0.00: two 300 mm treads, then the porch landing. The 0.90 m site reservation includes these treads and a lower approach allowance.
- Door-side drainage and a canopy gutter/outlet are coordination proposals. The terrace has a proposed fall to an outer collection channel and visible overflow at its C end, outside the entrance band; no drain is placed in the altar backing.

The plinth height is **not fixed** until road, yard, flood and drainage levels are established. Posts, footings, canopy/terrace roof, weather thresholds, slip resistance, guard infill and rain performance remain undesigned. No new standards/applicability claim is made in this revision.
