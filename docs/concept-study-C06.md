## Owner feedback and response

- **Bedroom exits:** C05 parents could leave only through the dressing nook and private bathroom lobby. C06 adds a separate 0.90 m bedroom door directly to the common hall. Brother also gains a direct 0.90 m common-passage door. Internal bathroom access remains separate. Former public doors into private lobbies are removed and passages enclosed from common circulation.
- **Reduce excess area:** F1 becomes 9.0 m along A × 12.0 m along B; F2 has the same envelope. All five bedrooms shrink. Rotating the private sanitary pairs retains 1.0/1.4 m clear widths and 2.0 m depths while reducing each suite passage from 7.20 to 3.12 m². The C-side upper envelope extension is removed.
- **Seating and altar:** The 2.20 × 0.85 m sofa faces +x/front/road; its rear faces B, away from the altar. A back line and facing arrow show that intent. TV sits beside the entry on the front wall, clear of the 1.90 m doorway. This implements the family's stated rule; no universal feng shui claim is introduced.
- **Left parking:** A 3.0 × 5.0 m stationary bay moves to the displayed left/C outdoor strip, approached along its long axis through a separate D vehicle gate. A separate pedestrian gate reaches the porch. The issued position preserves the kitchen–garden–porch walk.
- **Usability first:** [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) requires actual household activities, door operation, privacy, occupied furniture, storage and garden/parking access in every decision. Room containment alone is insufficient.

Shrinking the house does not resolve environmental performance. Survey/permissions, aircraft/road noise, structure, services and affordability remain open.
