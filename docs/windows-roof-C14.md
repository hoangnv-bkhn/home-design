## Boundary openings

**Bộ luật Dân sự 2015, 91/2015/QH13, Article 178(1):** the owner's right to put doors/windows towards neighboring buildings and shared paths is governed by construction law. It does not grant a right merely because a wall is 0.30 m from the boundary. Source: [National legal database / Ministry of Justice](https://vbpl.moj.gov.vn/bokhoahoccongnghe/Pages/vbpq-toanvan.aspx?ItemID=95942). The official indexed Article 178 text was retrieved; direct full-page fetch returned 502/403 in this session. No numerical setback or permission was calculated from search snippets.

**TCVN 9411:2012, Nhà ở liền kế — Tiêu chuẩn thiết kế:** the [official construction-standard catalogue](https://tieuchuanxaydung.vsqi.gov.vn/tieuchuan/view?sohieu=TCVN+9411:2012) lists it as active and scopes it to houses built in coordinated rows with shared architectural/infrastructure conditions. The full standard's boundary-opening clause was not obtained from an authoritative full-text source in this session. Do not impose an unverified 2 m rule or treat an exception as a permit. Applicability to this Noi Bai parcel, adopted local planning conditions, fire/privacy requirements and neighbor circumstances require local review. A TCVN catalogue entry is not itself a site-specific mandatory QCVN determination.

The owner reports a neighboring wall/house beyond A, with the house about **0.30 m away**; actual neighbor height, offset and rights are unknown. Project inference: such a slot can admit some sky light if sufficiently open above, but a tall adjacent wall can severely restrict it. It provides no proven air-change rate, lux value or cross-ventilation. Outward leaves and exterior cleaning are impractical in the modeled slot. High fixed glazing is still an opening and does not remove the permission question.

C14 shows three conditional A-side candidates in dashed pink: altar side light, parents high light and brother high light. They are recorded under `coordination.boundary_window_candidates`, excluded from the ordinary active `window_proposals/windows` schedules. No A/B window performance is used to justify the 2.40 × 2.00 m court or eliminate the normal operable court windows. If permission or daylight evidence rejects the altar side candidate, retain the opaque outer wall and study a borrowed lateral clerestory from living; that fallback is not currently drawn or claimed equivalent to external light/ventilation.

The owner's altar preference is a family design instruction. No new universal feng shui window rule or health/financial outcome is asserted.

## Stair window and assumed structure

## Solar hot water and hidden equipment

Primary technical source: [Solahart split MDV owner's guide and installation instructions, 122484, Rev-04, September 2025](https://solahart.com.au/sites/default/files/2025-09/install%20instruct%20-%20solahart%20split%20MDV%20electric%20mid%20-%20122484%20Rev-04%20-%202025%20Sept.pdf), printed page **40**, “Solar Collector Location,” “Roof Strength,” “Roof Area for Installation” and “Orientation of Solar Collectors.” Full PDF inspected, accessed 2026-10-04.

The manual requires an unshaded collector position, suitable roof support and product-dependent pipe/elevation arrangements; it recommends service clearance and discusses true-south orientation in the northern hemisphere. This is manufacturer guidance for that system, with Australian references; it is not a Vietnamese regulation or a specification for this house. No collector weight, capacity, orientation angle or service-clearance dimension from it is adopted as our system design.

Project inference: hide storage behind an inset ventilated screen while leaving collectors exposed, preferably exploring a split system. The sketch reserves both a cold-water tank and possible hot-water storage, but it selects no product, volume, pump or tank type. A thermosiphon alternative would need its integrated tank/collector arrangement coordinated afresh; it cannot be swapped into these boxes without review.

C14's screen top **8.15 m**, cold tank top **8.00 m**, hot-storage top **7.95 m**, **2.40 × 1.60 m** collector box and **0.80 m** maintenance strip are design placeholders. They are not manufacturer clearances or a load basis. Review actual equipment service/removal requirements, complete roof access/guard, supported loads, wind anchors, lightning/corrosion, insulation/hot-water controls, drain/overflow and seasonal self-shading. Structural support under the wet core is a proposal; no tank-bearing beam or foundation is designed.

Collector orientation in the drawing is schematic. The owner's 130° A bearing remains approximate; true north and the neighboring horizon must be established before sizing/orienting a system or promising output. Keeping the court open avoids covering it with equipment, but environmental performance still needs study. Airport proximity requires checking site-specific height applicability for the **complete equipment/screen envelope**; no height limit or exemption has been established.
