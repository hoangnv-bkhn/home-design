It provides no extra clothes storage; bedroom wardrobes remain. Beds shift 0.50 m toward B, retaining the ordinary door/wardrobe approach. The offset entrance wall blocks sampled bed views without a screen.

There is an explicit compromise: **72/324 sampled rays from the ordinary bedroom entrances see a sanitary doorway when all doors are open**. Removing a freestanding screen does not make all open-door views private. The family's privacy preference remains active and acceptance of this door-dependent solution is open.

## Sheltered terrace, ranch porch and parapet

Both options roof the shared **11.50 m² terrace**, retaining **1.00 m outward projection**, common access and open metal guards. Roof reservation **2.80 × 4.90 m** covers its plan; wind-driven rain is still possible. The terrace roof stays lower than the main roof as a light outdoor layer.

The ranch reference is interpreted as a broad horizontal canopy with slender supports, open sides and timber soffit. Porch frontage grows **2.20 → 3.30 m**, while depth stays **2.20 m**. Canopy grows **3.00 × 2.80 → 3.00 × 4.00 m**; its D projection stays the same and covers the existing two 300 mm treads. It is a compact arrival porch, not a furnished wraparound veranda. Three 150 mm rises/yard level remain assumptions. Garden access now turns toward the front steps; it does not require stepping onto the raised porch from its side.

The proposed **0.35 m low parapet** gives a common coping line at **6.95 m** without a tall boxed crown. It is an architectural edge, **not a maintenance guard**. The section shows a concept roof fall, outlet and independent overflow, with no sizing or construction specification. Local designers must resolve waterproofing/upstands, coping, insulation/condensation, drainage/overflow and protected maintenance routes. No new Vietnamese compliance claim is made.

General background: the Australian Government's [Your Home guidance on renovations](https://www.yourhome.gov.au/buy-build-renovate/renovations-and-additions) notes greater condensation risks for flat roofs and the need for specialist insulation advice; its [stormwater guidance](https://www.yourhome.gov.au/water/stormwater) explains the need to plan roof and site water together. Accessed 2026-10-04. These are general design references, not Vietnamese rules or a specified assembly. The porch/parapet recommendation is the assistant's design judgment for this model.

The inward court displaces the previous roof equipment screen. Cold/hot storage now reserves the **rear suite roof**, keeping collectors exposed and the hatch over the stair. This introduces tank-loading and noise concerns above a bedroom. Positions, capacities, framing/support, access/guards, replacement route, solar horizon and screening effectiveness remain open. Service screen top stays **8.15 m assumed**, above the low parapet; the parapet does not conceal all equipment by itself.
