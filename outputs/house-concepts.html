      Cook, sit, carry shopping

      The 1.6 × 0.7 m table has six occupied chair reservations. A 1.10 m counter aisle and 0.90 m garden approach are kept free. The left car bay connects to this walk and the porch. Real chair movement, appliances, levels and vehicle turning remain for detailed review.

      Bathrooms at the edge, a court at the center

      Private baths occupy the former court position on both floors. A 1.00 m private passage turns to separate WC and shower doors without a dressing screen. Parents and brother keep 3.80 × 4.00 m sleeping rooms and ordinary exits.

      The new court is 2.40 × 2.90 m. Direct bedroom windows reduce from 1.50 to 0.90 m; private-passage windows provide supplementary light. The gallery receives borrowed light through its open end. Actual daylight, privacy from all positions, extract ducts and drainage remain unresolved.

      A horizontal porch and one roof line

      The sister’s room returns to 14.80 m² with wider D/C windows, no cantilever and the same roof datum as the other rooms. Both options shelter the 11.50 m² shared terrace. A 3.30 m wide porch keeps its 2.20 m depth and open sides; compare slim and framed roof edges.

      F1 covered 116.16 m²; F2 enclosed 108.32 m²; terrace and 7.26 m² porch separate. Low parapet coping 6.95 m; service screen 8.15 m assumed.
