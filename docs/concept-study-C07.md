## Response to the owner's feedback

**Enter open space first.** The 1.90 m main opening moves 1.00 m toward C. Guests enter a **2.30 × 1.90 m furniture-free arrival reservation** beside the seating. They can pause and turn toward the sofa; the daily route follows the new 1.00 m open through hall toward kitchen/stair. No entry bench is introduced. The TV is centered on the 2.20 m sofa at source y=4.95 m, clear of the new door. Front-to-stand gap remains 2.25 m; actual viewing distance depends on seated eyes/screen size. Sofa rear stays away from the altar.

**Outward doors.** The owner's permission is implemented as two 0.95 m outward leaf studies. Porch depth grows from 1.50 to **2.20 m**, leaving a **1.15 m front waiting strip** beyond conservative sweep boxes. Steps, pedestrian gate and garden/arrival connectors move with the doorway. Levels, threshold, wind restraint, handles, weather protection and simultaneous passing remain open. The balcony shelters the entry and part of the porch; the front strip is not fully covered by its projection.

**Make the balcony part of the facade.** Both options have an independent **7.04 m² shared balcony** framed as the principal upper layer. Option 02 adds a shallower **2.60 m² private sister step-out balcony**, nominal 1.00 m projection. No shared route crosses her bedroom. This replaces C06's comparison between shared-only and bedroom-only corner access. The private addition is optional and adds cost/support/weather work; its net depth after guard can be tight. Assistant preference is the shared-only frame, not owner selection.

**Improve bedroom daylight.** Wider shaded openings are proposed toward the own yard: Grandpa 2.10 m, brother 2.30 m, sister 2.20 m; nominal height 1.65 m. Brother's wardrobe moves off the window wall. Sister's C wall remains for wardrobe, with a larger D opening separate from her optional balcony door. Bedroom 5 has a 1.12 m² diffusing rooflight candidate. Parents gain two explicit roof-tube routes through 0.40 m upper chase boxes. No A/B window or legal permission is assumed. See [research and limits](daylight-and-envelope-C07.md).
