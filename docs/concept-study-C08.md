## Altar and swap assessment

The altar remains on the front/A side, with a continuous backing wall and **0.90 m quiet indoor buffer**. A 0.60 m cabinet reservation leaves **2.40 m forward ceremonial depth**. F2 keeps the entire 10.20 m² projection empty, with a floor and occasional cleaning door; bedroom, furniture, wet zones and normal routes remain outside it. Its upper empty floor grows by 5.10 m²; that is a material space cost.

Swapping directly into Grandpa's front/C bay would put the altar's indoor backing buffer against the stair reservation and displace the upper sister room with the enlarged exclusion. A workable swap therefore needs stair and upper-floor relocation as well; it does not independently fix parents ventilation. C08 develops the simpler retained-side proposal. This assessment is not a fully drawn alternative or owner rejection of a swap.

The reviewed cultural sources establish no universal 3 m depth. This is a practical response to the owner's approximate suggestion, with no new feng shui prescriptions. See [research](altar-and-windows-C08.md). Existing facing, backing, seating and upper-exclusion family rules govern the proposal.

## Windows, court and site

Parents/brother receive normal 1.80 m opening windows to the **3.4 × 2.0 m own court**. No F2 floor or roof spans it. The court is separate from the altar backing buffer; normal windows can admit air when opened, but a single window wall and uncertain wind/neighbor heights do not establish air exchange or daylight. View is limited to the court. Ground maintenance access passes through the parents room, explicitly needing review; drainage, overflow, waterproof thresholds, roof edges, sound/privacy between floors and when-closed ventilation remain open.

Each storey has a **1.60 m stair window toward C**, with opening-panel candidates and floor-relative sill/height; final relationship to flights/landing/guards and hardware access needs design. Bedroom 5 now has a normal C window; tubes, upper boxes and its old rooflight are removed. Grandpa/sister D windows reduce to 1.20 m to reserve storage and optional balcony-door access. No A/B boundary opening is assumed. [Evidence and applicability](altar-and-windows-C08.md).

Both options retain independent shared BAL-01, 7.04 m². Option 02 adds sister BAL-02, 2.60 m² / 1.00 m nominal depth. Neither is selected. Guards, support, privacy, shading, usable depth and cost remain open.

The 2.20 m-deep porch, steps, outdoor waiting strip and garden walks move 1.50 m toward D with the enlarged house. Pedestrian gate and left/C stationary car bay are retained. Front walking connector narrows to a 1.00 m reservation; 1.15 m porch waiting depth remains. Actual maneuvering from the 3–4 m reported road, gate/car-door operation, levels and concurrent passing are unverified. Plot, offsets and 130° bearing retain their prior evidence status.
