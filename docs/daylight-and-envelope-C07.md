## The 30 cm gap

The owner asks whether a large bedroom window would help even with about 0.30 m to the neighboring wall. That gap is an owner placement preference; the neighbor's exact wall height/offset is unknown. A narrow gap might admit reflected light or sky light above the obstruction. Our geometric inference is that a tall close wall can leave little visible sky, so enlarging the glass alone cannot establish a bright room. The neighbor's future building also matters. No opening is added to A/B in C07.

Window placement, glare control and external shading matter alongside aperture size. The US Department of Energy describes these principles and identifies rooflights and solar tubes as ways to bring daylight into interior rooms. This is general building-science guidance, **not a Vietnamese regulatory or site-specific performance assessment**. [DOE Building Science Education — Natural Light and Daylighting](https://bsesc.energy.gov/energy-basics/natural-light-and-daylighting), accessed 2026-10-03.

Opening rights remain S02. The legal research starting points are **Bộ luật Dân sự 2015, 91/2015/QH13, Article 178** ([official database record](https://vbpl.vn/TW/Pages/vbpq-toanvan.aspx?ItemID=95942)) and **TCVN 9411:2012 — Nhà ở liên kế, design standard** ([VSQI catalog](https://tieuchuan.vsqi.gov.vn/tieuchuan/view?sohieu=TCVN+9411%3A2012)). The primary sites' full pages failed retrieval with 403/502 responses on 2026-10-03; search located their records, but complete authenticated clauses/current legal applicability were not verified in this session. No distance rule, exemption, legal permission or compliance calculation is taken from snippets. TCVN's applicability to this parcel/type and any incorporation into local requirements need a local architect's confirmation. A 0.30 m gap, fixed glazing, high-level glass or a neighbor's informal consent is not treated as approval here.

## Balcony and exterior composition

The shared balcony is the principal road-facing layer: **1.60 × 4.40 m, 7.04 m² nominal floor area**, shifted to align with the new entrance. Its common landing access stays independent in both options. Two side fins and a thin top plane create the modern/contemporary frame; materials, supports, guard design, solar shading and net finished usable area remain proposals.

Option 02 adds a **1.00 × 2.60 m private sister step-out balcony**, rather than giving up the shared balcony. Total nominal balcony floor area becomes **9.64 m²**. The shallower slab creates a secondary layer; its 1.00 m projection suits standing/planting more readily than seating. Guards/fixings reduce usable depth. Shared visitors do not cross the bedroom. The existing furniture-free front-side route reaches its door; chair/planter additions are not assumed clear.

Assistant preference: develop the shared-only frame first. The private addition is worth retaining as a comparison if the family values everyday step-out use; it is not needed merely to make the facade layered. Either option can also use recessed glazing, shade hoods and materials for depth. A drawn projection does not prove an engineered cantilever or authorization to build it. All projections are toward the modeled own yard, with no A/B cantilever; survey, planning/permit conditions and structural support remain unresolved.

The main balcony covers the entrance and part of the deeper porch. The front waiting strip extends beyond its plan projection, so full rain protection there is unresolved; the massing does not claim a completely roofed 2.20 m porch. Resolve this with the entrance/frame/weather detailing before fixing the facade.
