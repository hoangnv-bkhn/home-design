## Roof and porch alternatives

Keep stepped flat-roof volumes and the higher bedroom cap at 6.78 m. Reserve a **2.20 × 2.40 m ventilated service screen**, top **8.15 m assumed**, over the wet core. Separate cold-water and possible hot-storage footprints fit behind it, with a 0.80 m maintenance strip. A low **2.40 × 1.60 m collector reservation** stays outside the screen, altar projection and court. A possible pumped split solar system supports hiding storage separately; a thermosiphon assembly would need different tank/collector elevations. Equipment, capacity and product are unselected. The graphic is not a sized plumbing system or a solar-output study.

Reserve a ladder/hatch position over the stair and an independent roof approach. Safe hatch operation, headroom, guard/walkway, load capacity, replacement/lifting route, anchors, access-panel size, insulated pipe runs, hot-water controls, falls/outlets/overflows and airport height applicability remain open. The tank may be hidden from ordinary yard views by the screen; no visibility guarantee from neighboring upper floors is made. A seasonal sun/shadow survey must keep the screen/bedroom cap from shading the collector. The drawn collector slope/azimuth is schematic.

Both options cover the **2.20 m porch plus two 300 mm treads**, with the same 3.00 m canopy projection and assumed three 150 mm rises:

- **01 — blade canopy + open pergola:** 120 mm canopy edge, warm soffit and recessed 80 mm support reservations. Lighter appearance; pergola admits rain; canopy bending/fixity/supports need design.
- **02 — portal porch + sheltered terrace:** 300 mm portal edge, 160 mm framed support reservations, warm soffit and a short timber side-slat return. The return stays off the garden/arrival walks and outward leaves. Full terrace roof provides a sheltered sitting area, while supports, daylight, rain run-off and wind loads remain to resolve.

Assistant recommendation: develop **02's sheltered terrace/portal porch with the enclosed C-side bedroom extension**, while reviewing actual furniture and daylight. This is design judgment, not owner selection. The extra balcony/equipment/roof/canopy work remains a cost driver; affordability is unassessed.
