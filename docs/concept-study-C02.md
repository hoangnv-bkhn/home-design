## Arrival, rear kitchen and garden

Both house and altar face local +x, toward D's road, using the owner's 130° SE concept bearing along A. This is a proposed facade direction, not D's bearing/normal or surveyed north. After the requested display rotation this direction points down the drawing.

The F1 kitchen sits at the rear beside B. Its exterior door opens to owned land: F1 is moved to a proposed **1.8 m B-side gap**, keeping the assumed 0.8 m at A. This allows a narrow garden/path and door approach; it is not a deep rear lawn and grants no access to the neighbor's garden. The upper rear edge projects 0.8 m, leaving a modeled 1.0 m upper gap. Offset/opening rights, shading, drainage and survey remain unresolved.

Dining adjoins living, with a six-seat table and a lower circulation strip. Kitchen/dining connect through a **0.8 m opening and shared bathroom access lobby**. That indirect connection, the bathroom-door relationship, and living's 8.70 m² size are material compromises. A direct kitchen/dining connection is a useful next layout study if this route is unsuitable. Dining is adjacent to the rear kitchen rather than both forming a continuous rear room.

Parking moves to the C-side yard, with a 5.5 × 2.8 m stationary bay and three generic scooter/bike symbols. The car bay was moved after its first placement failed plot-corner containment. The final bay passes that limited check. Neither the car symbol nor the arrival line proves turning from the 3–4 m road, gate width, pedestrian clearance or delivery access.

## Suites, shower basins and stair

Parents and brother have larger sleeping rooms, offset bedroom/sanitary entries and an internal turning lobby. The suite entry and bedroom door are at the lobby's east end; WC/shower entries sit farther west, so the approach turns before entering either compartment. The solid shower side wall separates the reserve nook. Door leaves, bed sightlines and occupied fixture clearances remain unresolved; this arrangement is a concept privacy response, not a completed clearance study.

All four shower compartments include a 0.9 × 0.9 m shower tray and a basin within their own enclosure. Separate WC doors are retained, with small additional WC handwashing reservations. Fixture footprints fit and do not overlap in the model; user space, wet/dry separation, ventilation and door operation still need detailed design. All four wet compartments align floor-to-floor.

The owner confirmed staircase, 21 steps, 2.2 m area width and 26 cm tread depth. C02 interprets steps as **21 risers**, flights 10 + 11. At an assumed 3.3 m floor rise this gives **157.1 mm/riser**. Nine and ten intervening treads give runs 2.34 and 2.60 m; longest run plus 1.10 m intermediate landing fills the 3.70 m reservation. Two nominal 1.0 m flights and 0.2 m central gap fill its 2.2 m width. Wall/rail details may reduce finished widths. Top/bottom approaches are outside the bay in common circulation. The stair is offset from the entrance axis and turns sideways from arrival. No lift is requested.

These are geometric assumptions and arithmetic, not code suitability or stair safety findings. Slab opening, headroom, landing details, riser/tread convention and applicable requirements need architect-led development. No new standard-applicability claim or structural calculation is made in this revision.

## Altar and shared balcony

Altar width is measured **parallel to the backing wall (local y)**, not the rectangle's x dimension. The 3.4 m width is retained; 1.5 m ritual depth is a proposal requiring family review. The solid backing has a designated 1.4 m quiet indoor buffer immediately behind it, with side/end access. Wet rooms, stove and bedrooms are outside that designated buffer; the family's intended extent of “behind” remains to be reviewed.

F2 keeps precisely the new 5.10 m² altar projection empty and out of normal circulation. It has a slab and occasional cleaning-access opening, with no furniture. The former extension over the backing buffer is reclaimed as linen/utility. All bedroom, bathroom and balcony routes bypass the exclusion. Shrinking the zone does not adopt a universal feng shui minimum or approve the exact ceremony depth.

The road balcony is 1.6 × 4.4 m and opens from the common landing, without crossing a bedroom. Support, guards, waterproofing, sun/rain/noise and lawful projection remain unresolved. A shallow overhead frame is shown in the massing as an exterior proposal, not additional enclosed area or an engineered support system.

## Area and volumetric layering

Gross F1 120.96 m² + F2 envelope 129.60 m² = **250.56 m²**, with **7.04 m² balcony separately**. F2 envelope includes the 8.14 m² stair-opening reservation and 5.10 m² empty slab zone. These are concept envelope areas, not statutory, net or contract measurements. Unassigned extension/junction strips are reported explicitly in the [geometry schedule](../outputs/geometry-review.md).

The inferred site remains about 243.78 m². Model land outside F1 is about **122.82 m²**, including setbacks/gaps, parking and access rather than all planting. C01 offered about 143.78 m² outside its footprint, so C02 gives up about 20.96 m² of outdoor ground area. No registered area, legal coverage or affordability is established.

The massing uses a lower main volume, a rear upper layer projecting 0.8 m, and the shared road balcony/frame. This responds to the owner's modernism/contemporary preference through simple volumes; materials, roof/parapet, facade composition and any other projections remain unselected. Preliminary axes are discussion aids; engineer-led support/foundation design is needed for cantilever and balcony.
