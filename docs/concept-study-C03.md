## Yard and boundary consequences

Both floor rear edges are now near B, with about 0.30 m at A. Neither offset is a verified lawful setback or boundary-wall construction detail. Eaves, drainage, excavation/construction access, neighboring walls and opening rights require site-specific development.

Moving F1 by 1.7 m toward B and 0.5 m toward A concentrates outdoor space toward D/C. It does **not** increase total outdoor ground area: F1 stays **120.96 m²** and the inferred model still leaves about **122.82 m²** outside it. The C-side planting rectangle is only a concept reservation. Gate/access and stationary car/scooter symbols do not prove turning or safe passage. Part of the shared balcony projects over the car-bay edge; clear height and support locations are unverified.

C02's B-side garden door and rear cantilever would conflict with this close placement, so they are removed. The upper layer extends toward C, within the inferred plot. Garden access runs from living through the wider entrance and around the C-side yard. A direct kitchen/garden exit is **not resolved**; changing that adjacency requires another layout study rather than drawing a door into a 0.10 m boundary gap.

No A/B-facing door/window is assumed. Parents and the C-side rooms have candidate openings toward owned C/D space. The upper fifth bedroom moves to the rear/A corner with a **rooflight candidate**, not demonstrated daylight or ventilation. Kitchen daylight/extract and sized service routes remain unresolved. This revision does not claim those close-boundary spaces are ready to build or suitable without further design.

## Kitchen dining and TV arrangement

Compact kitchen dining is a useful direction for this brief: it frees the former central dining zone for living/altar use without increasing the footprint. The cooking and dining rectangles are editable subzones of one open 12.76 m² bay, not two enclosed rooms. Their 4.78 / 7.68 m² labels exclude a 0.29 m² open junction strip. A 1.6 × 0.8 m table and six small side-chair reservations retain the earlier approximately six-person dining brief. The latest request for a few chairs does not clearly cancel that count; comfortable occupied seating is still unverified.

Counter/fridge sit toward the rear B edge; the kitchen connects to the house through the existing shared access lobby. Dining no longer requires carrying food between separate rooms, but occupied chair/appliance space and the lobby relationship remain compromises for review.

The open living rectangle is 6.3 × 2.9 m, with a 2.2 m sofa opposite the TV stand on Grandpa's partition. The stand and sofa leave about **1.5 m between their front footprints**; this is not eye-to-screen distance. TV size, seating position, glare and finished room use remain unspecified. No TV product/size recommendation is made. Grandpa's door moves clear of the stand footprint. Main arrival passes through the open central floor; no coffee table is used in this study.

A 1.90 m entrance suits the desired broader arrival as a concept direction. Two nominal 0.95 m leaves are only a proposal; frames, finished clear opening, swing/slide hardware, porch/landing and furniture clearance remain unresolved. The drawing shows opening position, not a completed door design.

## Two balcony alternatives and layering

Both options use the same F1 and F2 interior room system. **01** keeps 1.6 × 4.4 m balcony off the common landing. **02** shifts it to the C/road corner, 1.6 × 4.7 m, accessed through the sister's room. The indoor shared landing remains in both; the alternative does not count that landing as additional balcony area.

The corner version visually joins the C-side upper layer more clearly and gives the sister direct outdoor space. It trades away independent shared access and affects her privacy. The shared version remains preferable if family-wide use takes priority. These are assistant judgments within the owner's conditional allowance; neither option is selected or guaranteed structurally feasible.

The simple massing compares the 0.8 m C-side upper volume, balcony/frame position, 1.9 m entrance and proposed openings/rooflight. Facade materials, roof form, guards and support details remain illustrative. There is no projection across B/A in the model; rights and survey accuracy are still unverified.

F1 envelope **120.96 m²** and F2 envelope **129.92 m²** sum to **250.88 m²**, with balcony **7.04 / 7.52 m² separately**. F2 envelope includes the 8.14 m² stair-opening reservation and empty altar slab zone; these are concept gross-envelope conventions, not statutory/contract/net areas. Unassigned junction/projection strips are disclosed in the [generated schedule](../outputs/geometry-review.md).

Parents remain **17.16 m²**, brother **20.68 m²**, Grandpa **15.60 m²**, sister **18.80 m²**, bedroom 5 **12.76 m²**. Both floors retain two separate WC/shower pairs. WC basins are removed; all shower basins remain. Wet compartments align vertically, and sanitary entries remain offset within private turning lobbies. Final door, user and fixture clearances are unverified.

Altar remains **3.4 m width × 1.5 m depth**, facing house/road SE with solid backing and a **1.4 m indoor buffer**. F2 keeps its exact **5.10 m²** projection empty, outside regular routes in both balcony options. Ritual depth and the family meaning of backing extent remain open; no cultural/standards rule was added.

The stair reservation remains **2.2 × 3.7 m**, 21 risers provisionally split 10 + 11, 260 mm going, 3.3 m assumed floor rise. Arithmetic/stacking does not verify headroom, opening, finished flights, landings or rails. Neither construction suitability nor a new standards-applicability claim is made.
